About 70 games

70 games is a curated arcade collection — 70 games, one website. No downloads, no accounts, no real ads. Featured spotlights at 20, 40, 60 break up the grid so it doesn't feel endless. The sidebars run top to bottom next to the games.

Built for fun, not for tracking. Favorites and recent plays are saved only in your browser.

Privacy

No data collected. No cookies for tracking. No analytics. Favorites, recents, and play counts are stored in localStorage on your device only.

If you share a game link, it only contains the game name in the URL (e.g. ?game=hotwire-maze).
